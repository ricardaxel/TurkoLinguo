# L10 — Locative -DA "in / at / on" (sample lesson, draft v0.1)

The first fully written lesson. It's meant to test the format before we fix the content schema and tech stack (see the JSON sketch in the appendix).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Unit | 2 — Where |

| Builds on | L2 harmony (`g.L2.harmony`), L3 *bu/bir* & no "is" in the 3rd person, L4 plural, L6 *değil*, L7 *mI* + *nerede*, L8 *var/yok*, L9 adjectives & numbers |

## Session 1 — Learn (≈ 3.5 min)

### Card 1 · Hook (10 s)
> 🐈 **Kedi nerede?** "Where is the cat?"
> 🛋️ **Kedi kanepede.** "The cat is on the sofa."
>
> Turkish doesn't say *on the sofa*. It adds an ending to the word: **kanepe → kanepe·de**.

Audio: both sentences, native speaker.

### Card 2 · Meaning (20 s)
**-DA = in, at, on**. One ending covers all three English prepositions.

| Turkish | English |
|---|---|
| ev·**de** | **at** home / **in** the house |
| masa·**da** | **on** the table |
| çanta·**da** | **in** the bag |

> 💡 There's no "is" in *Kedi kanepede*. As in L3, the 3rd person needs no verb "to be".

### Card 3 · The vowel: a or e? (30 s)
The **A** in -DA follows 2-way harmony (L2). Look at the **last vowel** of the word:

| Last vowel | Ending | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| a ı o u (back) | **-da** | oda·da, okul·da, banyo·da |
| e i ö ü (front) | **-de** | ev·de, bahçe·de, pencere·de |

> ⚠️ Only the **last** vowel counts: o·to·**bü**s → front → otobüs·**te** (not ~~otobüsta~~).

Interactive: tap the last vowel in *bilgisayar*, *kanepe*, *otobüs* → the 2×2 vowel chart from L2 lights up the matching side.

### Card 4 · The consonant: d or t? (40 s)
The **D** in -DA becomes **t** after a *voiceless* consonant. There are only 8 of them, hidden in the sentence:

> **F**ı**st**ı**kç**ı **Ş**a**h**a**p** — "Şahap the pistachio seller"
> → **f s t k ç ş h p**

| Word ends in… | Ending | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| a vowel | -da / -de | masa·da, kanepe·de |
| a consonant **not** in Fıstıkçı Şahap | -da / -de | okul·da, ev·de, çay·da |
| a consonant **in** Fıstıkçı Şahap | **-ta / -te** | kitap·**ta**, mutfak·**ta**, süt·**te**, otobüs·**te** |

> ✅ Good news: -DA starts with a consonant, so **the word itself never changes**: *kitap → kitapta*, *mutfak → mutfakta*. (Some later endings, such as "to" in L17, will make words change. Not this one.)

Audio button on the mnemonic (native speaker, slow then normal).

### Card 5 · Worked examples (40 s)
Each example animates in three steps: **word → choose vowel → choose consonant**.

| Word | Last vowel → A | Last sound → D | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| oda | **a** → a | vowel → d | **odada** "in the room" |
| ev | **e** → e | v → d | **evde** "at home" |
| dolap | **a** → a | **p** → t | **dolapta** "in the cupboard" |
| otobüs | **ü** → e | **s** → t | **otobüste** "on the bus" |

Plus one plural chain, segmented: **ev·ler·de** "in the houses". The plural comes first, then the location.

### Card 6 · Where? (15 s)
- **Nerede?** "Where?" — *Kitap nerede? — Kitap çantada.*
- **burada** "here" · **orada** "there" (these already contain *-da*: bura·da, ora·da)

### Easy items (6 × ≈ 10 s)
All of these use a word bank or multiple choice; no typing in Learn.

| # | Type | Prompt | Answer | If wrong → feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick the suffix | masa + ? (da / de / ta / te) | **da** | `vowel_back` + `after_vowel` |
| 2 | Pick the suffix | ev + ? | **de** | `vowel_front` |
| 3 | Pick the suffix | kitap + ? | **ta** | `voiceless` |
| 4 | Pick the suffix | bahçe + ? | **de** | `vowel_front` + `after_vowel` |
| 5 | Picture → choose | 🖼️ cat on a table: *Kedi …* (masada / masade / masata) | **masada** | per wrong option |
| 6 | Listen & choose | 🔊 "Telefon çantada." → which picture? (phone in bag / phone on sofa / phone on table) | phone in bag | replay slowly, highlight *çanta·da* |

End screen: "You can now say where things are. Next: practise it."

## Session 2 — Practise (≈ 5 min)

14 items, mixed with earlier grammar points (tagged so FSRS also gets review signal for them). From here on, typing is allowed, with the letter bar ç ğ ı ö ş ü İ.

| # | Type | Prompt | Expected answer(s) | Also reviews | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick the suffix | okul + ? | **da** | — | warm-up |
| 2 | Pick the suffix | otobüs + ? | **te** | — | trap: first vowel *o* is back, last vowel *ü* is front |
| 3 | Type the form | "in the kitchen" → mutfak… | **mutfakta** | — | shows `voiceless` feedback if *mutfakda* |
| 4 | Type the form | "in the milk" → süt… | **sütte** | — | t + te: a double t is correct |
| 5 | Build the word | tap: [ev] [de] [ler] | **ev·ler·de** | L4 plural | order check: plural before case |
| 6 | Translate EN→TR (word bank) | "The book is on the table." | **Kitap masada.** | L3 | distractors: *masade, var, bir* |
| 7 | Translate EN→TR (word bank) | "Where is the cat?" | **Kedi nerede?** | L7 | |
| 8 | Translate TR→EN | *Bahçede iki kedi var.* | "There are two cats in the garden." | L8, L9 | accept "There are 2 cats in the garden" |
| 9 | Spot the error | *Kalem masata.* | masata → **masada** | — | `after_vowel`: masa ends in a vowel → d |
| 10 | Spot the error | *Ali evda.* | evda → **evde** | — | `vowel_front` |
| 11 | Question → answer (type) | *Kitap çantada mı?* (🖼️ book on bed) | **Hayır, kitap yatakta.** | L7 *mI* | accept *Hayır, yatakta.* (dropped subject) |
| 12 | Listen & type (dictation) | 🔊 *Dolapta süt var mı?* | **Dolapta süt var mı?** | L7, L8 | accent near-miss rules apply |
| 13 | Picture scene | 🖼️ room scene: "Where is the lamp?" → *Lamba …* | **Lamba odada.** / **Lamba masada.** (whichever the scene shows) | — | scene asset, see "Assets" |
| 14 | Mini dialogue | A: *Telefon nerede?* B: *……* (🖼️ phone on sofa) A: *Teşekkürler!* | **Telefon kanepede.** / **Kanepede.** | T1 | ends on a set phrase |

End screen: score + "rule strength" bars for `g.L10.loc` sub-patterns (back/front vowel, voiceless, after vowel).

## Feedback templates

The form checker compares the learner's ending with the expected one and picks the matching template. Placeholders in `{}` are filled from the word's data.

| Code | When | Message |
|---|---|---|
| `vowel_back` | wrote *-de/-te*, last vowel is back | "Look at the last vowel of **{word}**: **{v}** is a back vowel (a ı o u) → **-{D}a**." |
| `vowel_front` | wrote *-da/-ta*, last vowel is front | "Look at the last vowel of **{word}**: **{v}** is a front vowel (e i ö ü) → **-{D}e**." |
| `last_not_first` | wrong vowel *and* the first vowel has the other harmony | add: "Only the **last** vowel counts, not the first one." |
| `voiceless` | wrote *d*, word ends in f s t k ç ş h p | "**{word}** ends in **{c}**, one of the Fıstıkçı Şahap letters → **t**: {answer}." |
| `not_voiceless` | wrote *t*, word ends in another consonant | "**{c}** is not in Fıstıkçı Şahap → **d**: {answer}." |
| `after_vowel` | wrote *t*, word ends in a vowel | "**{word}** ends in a vowel → always **d**: {answer}." |
| `detached` | wrote the ending as a separate word (*masa da*) | "The location ending is **attached** to the word: **{answer}**." (Sets up L13, where *de* "too" *is* written separately.) |
| `stem_changed` | changed the word (*kitabda, mutfağda*) | "-DA starts with a consonant, so **{word}** doesn't change: **{answer}**." |
| `accent` | right except for a missing accent (*bahce*) | "Almost! **ç**, not c: **{answer}**." (marked *almost right*, not correct) |
