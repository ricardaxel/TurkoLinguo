# TurkoLinguo — Vocabulary plan (A1, draft v0.1)

Companion to [CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md). The grammar lessons (L1–L35) are the **spine**. The vocabulary themes **hang off it**: each theme unlocks when the grammar needed to *use* its words has been taught.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Words come with a sentence frame.** A word is never taught alone. Each theme pack ships with 2–3 frames built only from grammar the learner already knows (e.g. Animals after L10: *Bahçede bir kedi var.* "There is a cat in the garden.").
2. **Pre-teach, then use.** A theme pack is placed just *before* the grammar lesson that will use its words. The lesson's examples then feel familiar, and the learner's attention goes to the rule rather than to new words.
3. **Spiral.** Each theme comes back in 2–3 later lessons with a new grammar point (food: *var/yok* → accusative → *-lI/-sIz* → *istemek*). Coming back is a better use of a session than adding more words.
4. **Core vs. elective.** *Core* themes are needed by the grammar lessons, and every learner gets them. *Elective* themes (animals, clothes, hobbies…) are chosen by the learner, for motivation and personal relevance.
5. **Frequency first.** Inside a theme, the most frequent words go into pack 1. We'll check this against a frequency list (Zargan / TNC, see [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md)).
6. **Morphology is part of the word card.** Each word stores its flags (softening, vowel drop, irregular harmony, see §5), so the form generator never produces ~~kitapı~~ or ~~oğulu~~. The card also shows them: *kitap → kitab·ı*.

### Sizes
- **Pack** = 8 words, learned in a single 3-minute **Words** session (see/hear → recognise → recall).
- **Theme** = 2–3 packs spread over the course (core pack early, extension packs later).
- A1 target: about **650 words** in total = about 250 words introduced inside grammar lessons (function words, core verbs) + about 400 in theme packs (≈ 50 packs).

## 2. Session mix

New session type: **Words** (3 min). A typical day might look like this:

| Day type | Sessions |
|---|---|
| Grammar day | Learn (L*n*) → Review |
| Practice day | Practise (L*n*) → Words (pack) → Review |
| Short day (3 min) | Review only, mixing vocabulary + grammar cards |

Rule of thumb: **about 1 Words session per grammar session**. A Words pack only becomes available when the learner has done the grammar lesson it depends on (the "unlock" column below).

## 4. Word lists (core pack + extension)

Flags: `[soft]` final consonant softens before a vowel, `[nosoft]` doesn't soften although the default rule says it should, `[drop]` vowel drop, `[front]` takes front-vowel suffixes despite a back last vowel, `[compound]` already ends in a possessive ending, `[double]` last consonant doubles, `[irr]` irregular. `[phrase]` = set phrase, shown unsegmented and never used to build new forms. See §5.
English glosses are short; the app will show images or emoji where possible.

## 5. Morphological flags (needed by the form engine)

The default rules (harmony on the last vowel; final p/ç/k soften before a vowel in polysyllables) cover most words. The exceptions must be stored on the word:

| Flag | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `soft` | p→b, ç→c, t→d, k→ğ (nk→ng) before a vowel | kitap→kitabı, ağaç→ağacı, renk→rengi, sokak→sokağı |
| `soft` (1-syllable exceptions) | most one-syllable words *don't* soften; these do | kurt→kurdu, tat→tadı, uç→ucu |
| `nosoft` (polysyllabic exceptions) | loanwords that keep the consonant | bilet→bileti, devlet→devleti |
| `drop` | the last vowel drops before a vowel-initial suffix | oğul→oğlu, burun→burnu, ağız→ağzı, şehir→şehri, isim→ismi, resim→resmi |
| `front` | front-vowel suffixes despite a back last vowel | saat→saati, saatte; kalp→kalbi; rol→rolü; alkol→alkolü |
| `compound` | the word already ends in a possessive ending (*-(s)I*): further endings follow the possessive pattern (buffer **n**), and in the plural the possessive ending moves to the end | yatak odası→yatak odasında, meyve suyu→meyve suyunu, buzdolabı→buzdolabını (plural buzdolapları), havalimanı→havalimanına, akşam yemeği→akşam yemeğinde, ev hanımı→ev hanımıyım, tereyağı→tereyağını |
| `double` | the last consonant doubles before a vowel (rare at A1, cheap to support) | hak→hakkı, his→hissi, sır→sırrı |
| `irr` | other irregular forms | su→suyu, suyun; ne→neyin (genitive), nesi; biz→bizim; verbs: yemek→yiyor, demek→diyor, gitmek→gidiyor, etmek→ediyor |

Note: *kahvaltı* and *ayakkabı* were compounds historically but now behave as ordinary words (*kahvaltıda, ayakkabılar*), so they take no flag.

Source: kaikki.org (Wiktionary) already contains the inflected forms for most nouns. We can derive the flags automatically and then hand-check the A1 list (about 650 words).

## 6. Media decisions (2026-10-20)

### Images → open-licence
- **Sources:** Wikimedia Commons and Openverse, filtered to **CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA** (no NC, no ND). **OpenMoji** (CC BY-SA 4.0) is the fallback for abstract words (*evet, lütfen, kolay*) and for a consistent icon style.
- **Avoid:** ARASAAC pictograms (CC BY-NC-SA, non-commercial only) and anything "free" without an explicit licence.
- **Every asset stores** `source_url, author, license, license_url`. The app gets a **Credits** screen generated from these fields (required by CC BY / BY-SA).
- Showing an unmodified CC BY-SA image does not force the app to be CC BY-SA. Only a modified image (cropped, recoloured) must be shared under the same licence.

### Audio → native recordings
- **Words: Lingua Libre (Wikimedia Commons).** 6,646 Turkish recordings, about 6,100 distinct words. Coverage check on our A1 list ([data/audio_coverage.tsv](data/audio_coverage.tsv), script [scripts/check_audio_coverage.py](scripts/check_audio_coverage.py)): **302 / 324 words (93%)**.
  - Missing: selam, görüşürüz, efendim, kafe, İngiliz, Amerika, Amerikalı, Almanca, İspanyol, İspanyolca, Japon, Japonca, poğaça, lacivert, salon, market, yıkanmak, kız, mont, nakit, poşet, rezervasyon.
  - To do: check each file's licence (mostly CC BY-SA 4.0, to be confirmed via the Commons API), choose one speaker per word when there are several, and normalise volume.
- **Sentences & dialogues:** open sources are thin. Tatoeba has only **about 1,100** Turkish sentences with audio, with a licence chosen per speaker (some NC). Common Voice is CC0, but its terms forbid redistributing the dataset, and quality varies.
  → **Plan:** have 1–2 native speakers record all lesson sentences and dialogues (A1 ≈ 1,000–1,500 sentences) with a written CC BY or work-for-hire agreement. The missing words above can be recorded in the same session, possibly through Lingua Libre to give back to the commons.
- **TTS (Piper)** is acceptable for ordinary words when no native recording exists. **Native recordings are required** for:
  - the L1 minimal pairs (*kır / kir, kız / kaz*);
  - T1 greetings and T1b survival phrases;
  - place names whose stress isn't on the last syllable (*İSTanbul, ANkara*);
  - *mI* question intonation;
  - words with **ğ**.
